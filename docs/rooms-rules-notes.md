# Rooms: Comprehensive Rules excerpts relied on

Source: official Comprehensive Rules text, media.wizards.com MagicCompRules 20251114 (downloaded and read in full; section 709 and 116.2m).

- 709.5: A shared type line represents two static abilities: "As long as this permanent doesn't have the 'left half unlocked' designation, it doesn't have the name, mana cost, or rules text of this object's left half" (same for right). So a locked door contributes NO name, mana cost or rules text. Types are shared (709.5a) so a Room with both doors locked is still an Enchantment - Room, with no name, no mana cost (mana value 0, colorless).
- 709.5b: the existence of each half is part of copiable values, even on the stack.
- 709.5c: "left half unlocked"/"right half unlocked" are designations of a permanent on the battlefield. A half without its designation is "locked".
- 709.5d: "given the 'left half unlocked' designation as it enters the battlefield if its left half was cast as a spell ... If it's entering the battlefield and neither half was cast as a spell, it enters with neither unlocked designation." (Redress Fate, reanimation, flicker: both locked.)
- 709.5e / 116.2m: "may pay the mana cost of a locked half ... to give that permanent the appropriate unlocked designation. This cost is referred to as an 'unlock cost.' This is a special action. A player can take this action any time they have priority and the stack is empty during a main phase of their turn." (Controller of the permanent; own main phase.)
- 709.5f: "unlock" by effect: choose a locked half, give it the designation. 709.5g: "lock" removes it.
- 709.5h: "Some abilities trigger when a player unlocks a particular half ... when that permanent is given the appropriate unlocked designation, regardless of whether ... while entering the battlefield or after."
- 709.5i: "fully unlocks": triggers when the permanent has one designation and gets the other, or has neither and gains both.
- 709.5j: a door is a half of the Room permanent.
- 205.3h: Room is an enchantment type.
- Zone change (400.7 new object): a Room that leaves and re-enters is a new object; designations do not persist.
